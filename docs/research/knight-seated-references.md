# Knight, seated: references and what they suggest

The question: the seated knight (round 9b) looks oddly proportioned: torso short, seat low, arms
stiff. How do other pixel artists draw a figure seated facing us?

Images are in `~/Projects/dark-forest-moodboard/refs/knight-seated/` (inspiration only); the board is
`shots/seated-refs-board2.png`.

## What ours does differently

Rough head-to-torso ratios (helm height : helm bottom to belt or lap), measured by eye off the board:
ours about 1 : 1.0, Solaire about 1 : 1.5, KBPixelArt about 1 : 2.

1. **The torso is short.** Every reference has at least half a head more torso than ours.
2. **The head sits on top, not in the shoulders.** In 2, 6 and 7 the helm sinks between raised
   shoulders or a collar; ours sits on a thin neck line, like a lollipop.
3. **The hips are no wider than the waist, and the seat is low.** In the references the lap (hips
   and foreshortened thighs) is the widest part of the lower body, and the seat sits about at knee
   height, so the butt reads as resting on something.
4. **He's upright and stiff.** The references lean a little forward, shoulders rounded, head a
   touch down: tired, at rest. That suits a vigil.
5. **The arms reach.** In the references the elbows go out and the forearms rest on something (the
   knees in 2 and 5, the thigh in 7, the hilt in 4). Ours holds the sword at arm's length.
6. **The log is a box.** KBPixelArt's log has its cut end on our right like ours, but its other end
   is rounded or hidden, and its top catches light.

## Ideas to try

- Extend the torso 3 to 4 rows (toward 1 : 1.5) and sink the helm 1 to 2 rows into the shoulders.
- Raise the seat: a slightly taller log, the hips flaring wider over it, short foreshortened thighs.
- Lean him forward a pixel or two, head slightly bowed.
- Arms: the sword hand on the pommel with the elbow bent, the other hand flat on the log beside his
  hip (the user's idea, which also shows he's sitting on it); or forearm across the thigh, as in 7.
- The log: round the left end, light its top, keep the rings on the right.
- Keep the shins thin (Sword & Sworcery).
