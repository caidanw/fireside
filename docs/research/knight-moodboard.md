# The knight: moodboard

References for redrawing the knight (small, cute, a bucket helm, his red cape kept), gathered
for inspiration only: nothing from them goes into the game. Images and a curated sheet are in
`moodboard/refs/knight/` and `shots/knight-moodboard.png`.

## What makes the cute ones cute

- **A big head.** The helm is about 40-45% of the figure's height (two to two-and-a-half heads
  tall), and a little wider than the shoulders. The realistic ones (KBPixelArt, GandalfHardcore)
  have small helms on tall bodies: handsome, not cute.
- **Rounded corners.** Even "bucket" helms read soft: the top corners are cut by a pixel or two,
  the top is slightly domed, and the bottom edge curves under the jaw. Hard boxes read as robots.
- **One simple face.** The visor is the face: a single wide slit, or a short grille of two or three
  dark vertical bars (Toko), sometimes under a brow band (lotus_garden). No breathing-hole
  freckles; small details become noise at this size.
- **A small plume** (Toko, Elthen, _Xeleph) adds charm and a second spot of colour: red, which
  ties to our cape.
- **A compact body.** Short legs, small hands, simple plate in two or three values; little
  surface detail.
- **Readable at a glance:** a clear silhouette, a few big shapes, strong value contrast between
  the steel and the cape.

## Directions to try (on the log-round stool, cape kept)

1. **Grille helm** (after Toko): a rounded bucket with two or three dark vertical bars and a tiny
   red plume.
2. **Visor helm** (after lotus_garden): a rounded helm with one wide horizontal slit under a
   darker brow band.
3. **Round helm** (after Elthen): a smaller, rounder helm with a T-shaped slit and a red plume.
